# Features in detail

The long descriptions moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (build 681) so every session starts lighter. Each paragraph is the full note as it stood; `CLAUDE.md` keeps a short pointer with the file names and identifiers. Keep both in step when a feature changes.

## Themes

The four looks beyond Auto, Light and Dark. The rule for every theme ("change everything, not just the main colours") and the one-theme mechanism stay in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Quest

**Quest** (App settings > Appearance, and the Quest alert style in Mini Fumu settings; id `quest2`; builds 499 and 500, the second attempt replaced the first; build 544 made it *the* Quest: the original plain Quest was removed from both lists and a saved choice of the old one now means this one, `applyTheme`, `applyLook`, `desktop/main.js`): the Quest look restyled as a game inventory (a real SVG-noise stone texture, thin metal frames, recessed dark slot rows with a light hairline, charcoal metal buttons with gold lettering, round metal-ringed icon buttons). It is the base Quest rules (`data-skin="quest"`, which Old School uses too, so they stay) plus `data-quest="slots"` on `<html>`, with its rules in one block at the end of `styles.css` (every selector starts `html:root[data-skin="quest"][data-quest="slots"]`); to drop it, delete that block and the `quest2` entry in `THEMES`.

## Old School

**Old School** (App settings > Appearance, and the Old School alert style in Mini Fumu settings; build 516): Old School RuneScape's interface: flat brown stone panels, thin black frames, square corners, yellow lettering with a hard black shadow (Pixelify Sans, the nearest free font to the game's; served from `fonts/`), orange titles, red picked tabs, green Add. It is Quest plus `data-quest="osrs"` with all its rules in one block at the end of `styles.css` (selectors start `html:root[data-skin="quest"][data-quest="osrs"]`); Mini Fumu's `al-osrs` (not `al-quest`: no fin) restyles cards and the ring, and his speech is the game's overhead text (yellow, black outline, no box). The settings window look is `html[data-look="osrs"]` in `desktop/panel.html`. To drop it, delete those blocks and the `osrs` entries (`THEMES`, `applyLook`, `alertStyle` lists in `main.js` and `panel-ui.js`).

## Bonfire

**Bonfire** (App settings > Appearance, and the Bonfire alert style in Mini Fumu settings; build 549): a dark fantasy-adventure menu look: near-black panels with a fine SVG-noise grain, thin muted-gold double lines, parchment lettering in EB Garamond (served from `fonts/`), gold titles with a hairline under them, quiet rows, and a glowing ember-orange for whatever is picked (tabs, tiles, today, switches, Add). It is Quest plus `data-quest="bonfire"` and **shares Old School's rules** (every `osrs` selector in `styles.css` is `:is([data-quest="osrs"], [data-quest="bonfire"])`, and `html:is(.al-osrs, .al-bonfire)` for Mini Fumu), so the Bonfire block at the end of `styles.css` only sets the palette (`--os-*` variables, kept under those names) and the looks that differ (fonts, gold lines, ember picks, the dark dressing-room stage, Profile cards, switches, his speech bubble as a small dark panel with a gold hairline, Mini Fumu's cards and ring), then a "details and polish" block: gold corner brackets on panels and cards (background gradients), a flame icon (`--bf-flame`) and a fading gold rule on titles, embers drifting up (`bf-ember`, on the stage's two sparkles), a slow pulsing glow on what is picked (`bf-glow`), a vignette on the room picture, and on Mini Fumu's cards a small square gold close button in the top-right corner (instead of the round one at the bottom), a gold rule under the title, a flickering flame, no corner brackets (they ran into the icon and the button) and the icon standing on a small round bronze pedestal (a pseudo-element on the bell's span, a background on the plain picture icons such as the note and reminder emoji, and a package picture drawn in the update card's empty first slot; the reminder icon rings by shaking sideways so its pedestal stays level). Keep that in mind when changing an Old School rule: it changes Bonfire too. The settings window look is `html[data-look="bonfire"]` in `desktop/panel.html`; `bonfire` is in the alert-style lists (`desktop/main.js`, `panel-ui.js`, `applyLook`, `applyTheme`). To drop it, delete the Bonfire blocks and entries.

## Sweet

**Sweet** (App settings > Appearance, and Mini Fumu settings > Theme; build 566): candy and strawberry milk: a pink page with white polka dots, puffy white cards with a pink outline, a ring and a soft edge under them, glossy pink pill buttons, pastel sticker icons, a few sprinkles. A light look with its own skin (`data-skin="sweet"`, `data-theme="light"`), all its rules in one block at the end of `styles.css`; the small window wears `al-sweet` (cards, bubble, ring), which existed before the app look. To drop it, delete the block and the `sweet` entries (`THEMES`, `desktop/main.js`, `desktop/panel-ui.js`).

## Dressing room, closet

`app-petsheet.js`: the pet's name, species and skin panel. `app-dress.js`: the Pet button's page, the dressing room: a stage on top, then tabs (`#dressTabs`: a row of six icon tabs, Pet, Hats, Clothes, Face (glasses and mouth things), Neck, Feet, whose icons are listed in `TAB_ICONS` in `tools/copy-emoji.js`; it always opens on Pet, whose panel holds the name, species, skin and personality; only the panel under the tabs scrolls; the stage rebuilds when the species or skin changes via `buildDressView`) (its stage is the `.dress-fx` SVG in `index.html`: podium, lights that sway, curtains, slowly pulsing sparkles, nothing moves about); the preview pet does little dances and poses every few seconds (`DRESS_MOVES`, the same CSS classes as the main pet), waiting while you try things on, and now and then poses for a photo (`PHOTO_MOVE`: `#dressFlash` and the `shutter` sound; also sometimes after putting an outfit on). The flash is a soft warm glow that eases in and out, at most one every 3.5 seconds, kept gentle for safety: keep it that way. `app-closet.js`: the closet, outfits saved by name in `pet.closet` (`L.saveOutfit`, `parseCloset`; up to `CLOSET_MAX`), opened by `#closetBtn` in the corner of the dressing room's stage; its sheet (`#closetSheet`) is opened with `show()` rather than `openDialog`, which would close the dressing room under it. `app-room.js`: room furniture. `app-goals.js`: goals and unlocks.

## Wrecking ball

`app-wreck.js`: the wrecking ball, a second toy (spiky black) picked with the toy switch in the ring menu (it replaced Pat; `toyKind`, `toggleToyKind`). Thrown in the small window with "Toy over the whole screen" on and awareness level 2 (`NEEDS.wreck`), it flies for 10 s (`wreckFling`); the open windows are solid, every time the ball meets a window it is sent off in a random direction away from it (it never flies across, and a plain bounce could trap it) and each hit cracks the cell it struck (each window is a grid, `gridFor` in `desktop/wreck.js`; the `glass` sound): `desktop/wreck.js` + `wreck.html` is a see-through click-through overlay that draws glass cracks and a few falling shards over the windows' rectangles, on a still layer (nothing is redrawn until something moves); no screen picture is taken, the overlay only knows the windows' rectangles (an earlier build captured the screen so falling pieces showed their content, and it stalled the PC); a 25 s watchdog removes it), then he casts `castWand` (with the `magic` sound) and the cracks fade away. Each hit gets one of several mark looks (`crackShapes`, `CRACK_TYPES`: web, bullet, long, mosaic, dent, chip; the same generator is in `app-wreck.js` and `desktop/wreck.html`: keep them in step). In the page itself (phone app, full app; not `html.desktop-pet`) a throw is `pageWreck` instead: the ball flies over the whole page and the page's own list rows, aisle labels, add bar, dock buttons, gear and lamp (`WRECK_TARGETS`) crack (the border breaks first, then picture, words and bar; a ball that has taken half of a thing passes through the rest so it cannot get stuck, and a hit near an existing crack adds no new one; the marks are drawn on the element itself, clipped to it, and every shard carries its part) and then break piece by piece like bricks in Breakout: the ball bounces off a thing and each hit adds a crack and chips off a few pieces (`chip`; a row: picture, words, then the bar), so it takes about eight hits to break one thing: each triangular shard is a frozen-style copy of the element cut with `clip-path` and animated with Web Animations, the element itself is cut with `clip-path: path(evenodd)`, nothing is captured, a see-through shield blocks taps, and the wand makes them pop back. The toy switch for it is a row in App settings (Options), since the ring menu is desktop-only. New desktop files must be listed in `desktop/package.json` `files`.

## Laser dot

`app-laser.js` (build 695, reworked in 699): the third toy, a little pink laser pen, picked in the ring menu's toy circle (Laser dot; `toyKind` `laser`, `stage.toy-laser`). Picking it there starts a chase at once, and so does tapping the pen; the main ring has no toy buttons. A chase lasts `LASER_MS` (20 s). The dot is a bright core that flickers and trembles inside a breathing glow (`.laser-dot`, the same look in Mini Fumu's dot window); a fast dot leaves a short streak in the page (`.laser-trail`).

He plays it like a cat (`laserRule` in the page, `place.laserPhase` in the shell, the same rules, tested in `tests/desktop.test.js`): **chase** while it moves (behind and below it, so it is in front of his face; he leans into the flight, `--lean`, flaps in the air and runs on the floor), **stalk** once it has sat still near him for a moment (he backs off low and wiggles his bottom, squinting), **pounce** after about a second (a quick leap that lands with the dot just in front of him, so the pointer is not over him), **caught** (kneading it, happy, a line and hearts; a slow drag takes him along with it, switching sides at an edge) until it darts away, then "hey, come back!" and the chase again. His eyes follow the dot all the time. When the time is up the dot goes out and he looks round for it (**lost**: looking left and right with a puzzled tilt and a "?"), then goes home out of breath (`laserTired`: the laser moves come off so his walk home is his ordinary walk, and the drop keeps that face).

In Mini Fumu (wander mode only; the ring leaves it out in room mode) the shell flies his window after the mouse anywhere on the screens every 8 ms (`desk:chaseStart` in `desktop/main.js`, `place.chaseStep`, never off the screens or below the floor), draws the dot in the toy window (clicks pass through it) and tells the page the part of the game, his speed, which way he faces and where the dot is from his face (`onChase`); after the look round he drops to the floor and runs back (`throwWindow` without spin). Clicking him ends it sooner (`chaseStop`, no look round). It uses only the pointer's position, so no awareness level is needed. Walks, peeks and perches wait while it runs. In the page (phone, full app) a copy of him (`.laser-ghost`, like the sneaky snack's trip) flies over the whole page after the finger or mouse (`laserStep`), a see-through shield (`.laser-shield`) takes the taps; tapping him catches him, Escape or 6 s without moving ends it. Animation player: laser dot chase, laser: stalk and wiggle, pounce, caught it (kneading), where did it go? (`laserDemo`). Only a game: nothing counts for goals.

## Sneaky snack

`app-steal.js`: the sneaky snack. Each day he starts quick (the first one 20 to 35 s after the app opens, then every 45 to 110 s) until you catch him and he spits one out (`nibble-steal-caught` keeps the day); after that it is rare (8 to 16 minutes) until tomorrow (`scheduleSteal`, only when `stealOk`: the app, shopping list, awake, nothing else going on) Fumu steals the emoji off one visible shopping row (`stealNow`): a copy of him (`stealTrip`: a fixed `.steal-ghost` stage with a cloned pet; the real one is hidden meanwhile) walks down over the page with one paw out, takes the emoji, runs back fast, and then the real one eats it; the row keeps its text, only the picture is hidden (`.item.stolen`, put back after every list redraw by `applyStolen`) until you poke him: embarrassed, he spits it back (`stealSpit`). Nothing is saved; a reload brings the pictures back. Dev tool: Make Fumu steal an emoji (`devSteal`); also in the animation player.

## Celebrations

`app-birthday.js`, `app-xmas.js`, `app-halloween.js`, `app-newyear.js`, `app-easter.js` and `app-kingsday.js`: the birthday celebration (the player's birthday), the Christmas one (25 December), the Halloween one (31 October), the New Year's Day one (1 January), the Easter one (Easter Sunday) and the King's Day one (27 April, the 26th when that is a Sunday; `notSunday` in `bonuses.js`): each plays once, the first time the app opens that day, before alerts and other animations (`birthdayHolding()` holds messages and reminders back, `busy` holds his own moves, the toy is put away and pops back in): a hat, the party dance (`partyDance` in `app-idle.js`), a tune (`birthday`, `christmas` in `sounds.js`), the app's banner or Mini Fumu's bubble, and at the end a cake or a gift box . The birthday has fireworks, Christmas snow and lights, Halloween bats, ghosts, webs and mist (the string of lights is `xmasLights`, shared). Halloween ends with a trick-or-treat bucket. New Year's Day has a disco ball, the disco dance (`discoDance`, with the `disco` arm pose) and a party blower. Their dances come from beat tables (`danceCss`, `danceSquish` in `app-idle.js`). Easter has bunny ears (a flower crown on a bunny), the bunny dance (`bunnyDance`), pastel flags, petals, butterflies, hopping eggs and a chick hatching from an egg . Effects are drawn after the card so they pass over it. King's Day goes orange: a crown, the royal dance (`kingsDance`, with the `royal` arm pose), orange and Dutch-flag bunting, balloons, confetti, a brass-band polka, and a tompouce with a flag. Each celebration counts its own hold in `celebrationHolds()` so two pending on one day do not block each other. In Mini Fumu (`html.desktop-pet`) a celebration does not start by itself: an alert card asks first (`celebAsk` in `app-birthday.js`: Celebrate / Not now; the day counts as offered either way, the card leaves after 2 minutes, alerts wait while it is up), and when she says yes the effects cover the whole screen: the page asks the shell (`celebrateFx` in `desktop/preload.js`, `desktop/celebrate.js`) to open a transparent click-through overlay window that loads `celebrate.html` from the web, which runs the celebration files' own effect functions (`celebrate-fx.js`; the files' start-up code stays quiet there because the stand-in date is made-up) while the page in his window plays the dance, hat, music and gift. An installer without `celebrateFx` keeps the old in-window effects (`celebFx` returns false). The special gift (cake, box, treat, egg) comes once a year per kind (`yearGift`, `nibble-gift-<kind>` on the device; the developer tools may hand it out again). The Developer tools play all six.

## Gift days tab

`app-shopcal.js`: the Shop sheet's first tab, "Gift days" (the Shop is the other tab; a temporary home for it): a month grid of the special days from `bonuses.js` (dots for a celebration and for extra gift boxes), the tapped day's details and a coming-up list. A `DAYS` entry's `party` names the celebration Fumu plays that day. It is meant to carry login bonuses and such later; it changes no data.

## Weather

`weather.js` (pure, tested) and `app-weather.js` (build 633): Fumu reacts to the weather outside. Off until she turns it on in Options > Weather and gives a place (a typed city, looked up on open-meteo.com, or the device's position rounded to 0.1 degree; only that is sent; kept on the device as `nibble-weather`, shared by Mini Fumu and the whole app through the same storage). `Weather.classify` turns a WMO code and the temperature into clear, rain, storm, snow, cold or hot; the page fetches every 30 min (`wxFetch`) and `wxSet` draws it: an umbrella over his head (rain, storm; in the hat layer so outfit redraws keep it), the winter scarf from the wardrobe (cold, snow, unless he wears one), a paper fan and a sweat drop (hot), falling drops, flakes or a sun over the stage (`.wx-fx`) (Mini Fumu shows no falling rain drops since build 726; the splashes on his leaf stay), a shiver now and then when cold, and one line a day. Everything is hidden at bedtime. Developer tools: Weather pretend-each-kind; the animation player has a Weather group. He also says, once a day per kind, when special weather (rain, storm, snow, heat, cold) is coming later today (`Weather.laterToday` reads the hourly forecast, `wxLater`; the ring's report adds it in a second bubble, `ringLater`). The setting is kept by the Windows shell too (`weather` pref `{on, lat, lon, place}`, `desktop/weather-place.js`, `setWeather` in `preload.js`, `wxFromShell` in `app-weather.js`) so Mini Fumu settings > Behaviour > Weather and the app's Options > Weather are one setting; the settings window finds a place through the shell (`weatherFind`), since it has no network of its own.

## Background sounds

`app-ambience.js` (build 677): background sounds, quiet synthesised sounds now and then (never a loop) that fit the background behind him (`AMBIENT_BACKDROP`: waves and gull, bubbles and blub, birds and breeze, hen and cow, sizzle and clock, crickets and owl) and the weather (`AMBIENT_WEATHER`: thunder in a storm, wind, cicadas; no rain sound since build 726); the sounds themselves are in `sounds.js`. They obey Quiet mode and Mini Fumu's volume, stop while hidden, with a sheet open or in bed, and are switched off by Options > Background sounds (`state.settings.ambience`) or Mini Fumu settings > Background sounds (the `ambient` pref, once "Night sounds"). In Mini Fumu the backdrop sounds play only in room mode.

## Plant

`app-plant.js` (build 633): the little plant, room furniture (`plant` in `decor.js`, drawn by `PlantArt.svg(stage, flower, thirsty)`: a pot with a face, seven stages from seed to flower). Since build 677 it is ordinary furniture: nothing puts it in the room by itself (place it from the Room list, drag it about), it shows on both lists and in Mini Fumu's room, and the ring menu has no plant button; the first task ticked off each day (`plantWater` from `toggle`, also when a reminder is marked done on Mini Fumu's card) still waters it, whether or not it is placed, and it grows a stage (`pet.plant` = `{days, last}`, a counter that syncs by taking the larger values; `L.plantInfo`, `L.waterPlant`); it never dies, waits when a day is missed and droops after two. At the flower Fumu shows it off (`plantProud`); the next watering picks it ("You picked a flower!") and plants a seed with a different colour. Tap it for a line from Fumu. Developer tools: water it now (places it if needed), start over. The room's other furniture: rug, window, beanbag, cuckoo clock (real time), gaming desk, bookshelf, floor lamp, fish bowl (`decor.js`; the burger phone was removed in build 677, an older room that has one just no longer shows it).

## Holiday hats and gift boxes

`bonuses.js` (plain data and pure functions, like `decor.js`): the holiday gift boxes' calendar: `BOXES` (how a box looks), `DAYS` (holidays and the birthday; add a line to add a day; a day with a `party` also has a `hat`, `hatLabel`, `short` and `box`). **Holiday hats (build 662):** the hat Fumu wears in a celebration (party hat, Santa hat, witch hat, New Year hat, Easter ears, golden crown: `gift: true` items in `wardrobe.js`) comes in a gift box on that day: using the app or Mini Fumu on it puts one box in Profile (a count on the Profile button, a toast once a day, `giftOpenToast`), opening it unlocks the hat (kept in `pet.prizes`, which syncs; `unlocked` in `app-goals.js` ignores `FreeUnlocks.all` for gift hats and the lock badge shows 🎁 and the holiday). There are no daily boxes and no other prizes. `app-gift.js`: the Profile gift sheet (`pet.gifts`, `pet.prizes`; dev tools to pretend a special day). `app-profile.js`: Profile (`state.player`: name and birthday, the Profile button at the end of the dock on both lists; its sheet also holds the buttons that open the Top 10 and the Stamp book).

## Room mode and wander mode

**Room mode and wander mode** (Mini Fumu settings > His look > Mode; pref `deskMode`, `room` or `wander`; new installs start in room mode, an older save with a room background keeps room mode and any other becomes wander): room mode shows his room (`backdrop` follows it; the cushion is a switch in room mode, `hideCushion`, and always hidden in wander mode) and keeps him in it (his movement choices become A little / In his room / Still, he never sits on windows or peeks round the screen, and the toy and wrecking ball stay inside his window: `toyRoamOn()` in `main.js` is false); wander mode removes the background and cushion, lets him walk about the desktop (A lot / Normal / A little / Still, Sits on my windows) and lets the toy fly over the whole screen (the "Toy flies around the screen" switch, `toyRoam`, shows only here). The shell sends the page the derived `backdrop`, `hideCushion`, `perch` and `toyRoam` plus `deskMode`; `moveLevel` in `app-desktop.js` turns a lot or normal movement into `room` in room mode.

## Swipes and the ring menu

The ring's buttons sit evenly spaced along one curve over his head (build 698, `build` in `app-ring.js`): with too little room above him the curve gets flatter, near the side of the screen the buttons move round it and then the whole curve slides; no button is pushed against an edge on its own.

Reminder and alert cards: swiping (drag, or sweeping the pointer while the mouse passes through him in a game) does, by default, left = Done / copy, up or down = In 10 min, right = put away; each of the four directions has exactly one action the player can choose in Mini Fumu's settings (Behaviour > Swiping alerts: Done, In 10 min, Put away, Nothing; prefs `swipeLeft`, `swipeRight`, `swipeUp`, `swipeDown`; `swipeMap` and `swipeResolve` in `app-send.js` work out what a swipe does, used by `sweepAt` in `app-desktop.js` and the drag code in `app-send.js`). Ctrl+Alt+G (`grab`) swaps pass-through and solid in games. The ring menu opens with a double click on him (`app-ring.js`; Mini Fumu's buttons: a Toy circle (opens a second ring in place of the first: No toy, Default toy, Wrecking ball, Laser dot (those two left out in Mini Fumu's room mode, `deskRoomMode`), and a Back button; `setToyKind`; picking Laser dot starts a chase; the main ring has no toy buttons since build 699), snack, a Send button (`ring.send`, only with Backup & sync on: in Mini Fumu `sendCopiedNow` in `preload.js` makes the shell read the clipboard like the Ctrl+Alt+S key, in the app it opens the Send sheet), a Weather report that is offered only while the weather option is on (`ringWeather`: he looks the weather up for the saved place and says it; with no place picked he says to pick one in Options > Weather), swap list; the dance and wave buttons were removed in build 665); in the app (phone, full app) a double tap opens the same ring with the Toy circle (all three choices), the snack, the plant on the to-do list, and the Weather report while the weather option is on.

## Trip stamp

**Trip stamp** (build 582): buying the last item on the shopping list earns a trip stamp, the Trips card at the front of the Stamp Book (kept as `stamps.trip`, so it syncs like the task stamps; `L.recordTripStamp` in `logic.js`: the trip goal's rules, at least `TRIP_MIN_ITEMS` items that sat on the list 15 min and the clock in order, and one a day, `guard.trip` holds the day). In `celebrate` (`app-actions.js`) the stamp thumps onto the receipt first (`tripStampAt`, `.trip-stamp`, a coral rubber-stamp print; over his head where there is no receipt), a toast says "Trip #N stamped!", then the usual party with a trip line. A short list (under 3 items) still gets the party, just no stamp. The Stamp Book opens from Profile on both lists.

## The desktop pet's five scenes

**The desktop pet's five scenes** (`L.deskScene`, `petScene()` in `app-night.js`, also `html[data-scene]`): `day`, `day-clip` (daytime on the to-do list: the clipboard in his left hand), `night-bed` (his bed is out; asleep or tucked; the toy and clipboard are put away), `night-drowsy` (up at night, e.g. from using the shopping list, or woken by the bell) and `night-drowsy-clip` (the same on the to-do list). Every new desktop animation or interaction must work in all five: check the clipboard hand (use the right hand for anything held when the clipboard is out: wand, toy), that nothing wakes or startles him in `night-bed` (alerts and reminders only show their card there), and that bed-only things (night play in `app-night.js`, the bed turning when thrown) apply only while he lies in his bed (`inBed()`). In the small window and in the app (phone and full app) at night, in bed, the toy is replaced by a bell (`.bell`; in the app breaking it also puts the lamp out and tucks him in): shake it (the first ring stirs him, the next wakes him up drowsy until morning, `bellAwake()`), or throw it and it breaks and he goes back to bed (a new one turns up a few seconds later).
