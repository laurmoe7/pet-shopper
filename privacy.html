Fumufumu and your data

Last updated 20 October 2026. This page says in plain words what the app keeps, where, and how to delete it.

On your device

Fumufumu keeps your shopping list, your to-do list, your pet (its name, looks, room, outfits and progress), your options, and your name and birthday if you type them in. All of it stays on your phone or computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on Backup & sync.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app loads no fonts, scripts or pictures from other companies.

Backup & sync (only if you turn it on)

  • What is sent: your shopping list, your to-do list (with your calendar plans), your pet, and your name and birthday. Only your own devices with your code see them.
  • Where: a small server run for this app on Cloudflare. It keeps one copy per backup code, and the code itself only as a hash. There is no account name, e-mail or password.
  • The code is the only key. Anyone who has it can see and change your lists and pet, so keep it to yourself.
  • A household (Options > Devices > Household) shares one shopping list with the people you invite. They see that list, who added and ticked its items, and each other's names from Profile, never your own lists, to-dos or pet. Its owner can remove people. A household not used for a year is deleted.
  • Notes and links you send between your own devices wait on the server for at most a day, then they are deleted.
  • When you start a backup, the server keeps a scrambled form of your connection address for one hour, only to stop someone from making thousands of backups at once.
  • Delete: Options > Devices > Backup & sync > Delete my backup removes your backup from the server at once and takes you out of your household. What you put on a household's shared list stays there for the others until they remove it. A backup that has not changed for a year is deleted by itself.
  • Download: Options > Devices > Backup & sync > Download my data gives you a file with everything the app has.

Recipe links

When you paste a recipe link, the app sends that link to a small helper on Cloudflare, which reads the page and passes back only the recipe data. It keeps nothing; Cloudflare may hold a copy of the page for ten minutes to answer faster.

Adding by voice

The microphone button uses your browser's own speech recognition. In Chrome that means the audio is sent to Google to be turned into words, under Google's terms. Nothing is recorded by Fumufumu.

Updates

Now and then the app asks this site which version is newest. The Android app also asks GitHub, at most every few hours, whether a newer Android app is out; GitHub sees your connection address, under GitHub's terms. Nothing else is sent.

Phone alerts (Android app)

A to-do with a time gets an alert on your phone. The phone itself schedules it; nothing is sent to a server. Turn it off in Options > Phone alerts.

Mini Fumu on Windows

The desktop pet never reads window titles, page text or your screen. At the Normal privacy level it knows only the file name of the program in front (matched against a short list of games and common apps) and where your windows are, so he can sit on them. At the More privacy level he knows only whether you are at the keyboard and the time. Nothing about this leaves your computer.
